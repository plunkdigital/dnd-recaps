# Icewind Dale Campaign: Character Summaries

Based on *Icewind Dale Campaign Recap: Full Session Summary*, covering Sessions 1 to 3. The sorcerer's name is standardised as **Azlor** throughout, as confirmed by the user.

## Main characters

### Orlen “Cricket” Vex

**Rock gnome fighter, guided by an unseen companion called Crick.**

Orlen is the party’s instinctive, emotionally driven fighter, showing bursts of bravery alongside a strong attachment to the creatures he befriends. His encounter with the harmless Chwingas is particularly revealing: he bonds with them, then nearly attacks Azlor after the sorcerer incinerates one. He ultimately restrains himself, but the incident creates a serious fracture in the party’s trust.

He also proves resourceful under pressure. During the dragon encounter, he improvises a parachute and lands safely in the snow. Despite his earlier conflict with the sorcerer, he subsequently drags the critically injured Azlor into East Haven. By Session 3, he can use **Giant’s Might** to grow to a large size, becoming a particularly effective melee fighter during the Water Weird battle.

His defining relationships are his mysterious connection to Crick, his strained relationship with the sorcerer, and his place alongside Balder in confronting wrongdoing. In East Haven, Orlen and Balder together expose Esmeraldor as Sefik Kaltro.[^orlen]

### Azlor

**Noble-born sorcerer with chaotic powers and telepathic gifts.**

The sorcerer is portrayed as **proud, calculating, ambitious and capable of ruthlessness**. Orlen and Balder initially venture into the frozen wilderness to rescue him, finding him strangely unfazed by the blizzard. His decision to incinerate a harmless Chwinga establishes the darker side of his character and places him in direct conflict with Orlen, with Balder attempting to mediate. In combat, his recorded abilities include **Chaos Bolt** and destructive fire magic.

He develops a low-key magical rivalry with Jack Carrasien, but his most consequential decision is his headfirst leap from the ancient ice dragon. He survives in critical condition and is brought into East Haven by Orlen. **The recap does not describe his recovery or establish a further active role for him in Session 3.**[^azlor]

### Balder Enginnson

**Dwarven cleric of Tyr, healer, mediator and increasingly influential religious figure.**

Balder’s character centres on **faith, compassion, balance and justice**. Early on, he adopts a wolf pup after finding the frozen Goliath woman who died protecting it. He also approaches a goblin and ogre camp diplomatically, speaking Orcish and invoking Tyr to secure much-needed food. When conflict erupts between Orlen and Azlor, he attempts to intervene rather than let the party turn on itself.

His healing earns him a heroic reputation in Lonelywood, where he cures disease and blindness. However, he is not simply a passive peacemaker: in the mine, he uses **Command** to send a kobold to its death. He also becomes temporarily possessed by **Yilthowral**, a power-seeking spirit, before the party burns the spirit’s pouch and releases it.

By Session 3, Balder combines genuine religious conviction with theatrical showmanship. A **Thaumaturgy**-assisted display inspires kobolds to worship and follow him. He uses **Sacred Flame** against the Water Weird and **Radiance of the Dawn** against the frost giant skeleton, then helps expose Esmeraldor and commands him to grovel during the public confrontation. His arc moves from travelling healer and mediator towards a figure with substantial local influence and devoted followers.[^balder]

### Jack Carrasien

**Flamboyant wizard from Luskan, possibly affiliated with the Arcane Brotherhood.**

Jack joins in Session 2 as a charming but suspicious arcane ally. His entrance is characterised by flashy magic, and he quickly develops a quiet rivalry with Azlor. His possible Arcane Brotherhood connection adds intrigue, but **the recap does not confirm that affiliation or explain his underlying motives**.

His defining recorded moment comes in the Termalaine mine: he falls from a cliff, casts **Feather Fall** to save himself, and abandons the party. This sharply undercuts his promising introduction and raises questions about his reliability. The recap does not explain his subsequent whereabouts or give him a recorded role in Session 3.[^jack]

### Thaddeus Hardius

**Half-elf paladin, noble and valiant, with a strong sense of duty.**

Thaddeus joins the party in East Haven while searching for a **noble quest**. Unlike Esmeraldor, he is presented as a straightforwardly honourable adventurer. His introduction is practical rather than elaborate: he stocks up on rations and trout before accompanying the expedition across Lake Dinneshere.

He attempts ranged support during the Water Weird encounter, then makes a much stronger impression against the frost giant skeleton, landing a **natural 20 with his longsword for 17 damage**. The recap establishes him as a dutiful martial ally, but provides little personal history and does not specify his paladin oath, deity or deeper motivations beyond seeking a worthy quest.[^thaddeus]

### Esmeraldor Longfeast, also known as Sefik Kaltro

**Smooth-talking potion hustler and flamboyant rogue who becomes an ally-turned-antagonist.**

Esmeraldor is an eccentric, persuasive and deeply suspicious figure. He introduces himself with knowledge of the **Bottomless Cauldron of Plenty**, drawing Orlen and Balder into the search through a shack filled with conspiracy boards and notes. His expertise makes him useful, but the recap describes his credentials as dubious from the outset.

His behaviour becomes increasingly evasive. During the Water Weird fight, he uses **Misty Step** to move away, with Balder’s kobolds following to keep watch. Later, the kobolds report that he has stolen the cauldron and fled during the party’s long rest.

Back in East Haven, he uses the cauldron to manufacture a heroic public image, selling food for a silver per serving, displaying banners of himself and ousting the mayor. Orlen and Balder expose him as **Sefik Kaltro, the murder suspect mentioned in Session 1**. His support collapses, he is arrested, and his Ring of Frost and Frost Dagger are confiscated. The recap establishes his deception and betrayal, but identifies him as a murder *suspect*, rather than documenting proof of the earlier killings.[^esmeraldor]

## Companions and supporting figures

### Crick

**The unseen presence associated with Orlen.**

Crick guides Orlen and helps explain his unusual behaviour. The recap leaves open whether Crick is an imaginary friend or a supernatural entity; Balder and Azlor discuss the mystery during an unsettled night’s rest. There is no confirmed origin, physical form or independent agenda.[^crick]

### Timmy

**A talking, berry-producing bush that feeds on blood.**

The party meets Timmy in the ancient tomb during Session 1, and he becomes a travelling companion. He is described as comical and useful, with a high-pitched, sassy manner of speaking. His blood-fed berries are his principal unusual feature; the recap does not establish a deeper backstory or personal objective.[^timmy]

### Balder’s wolf pup

**A rescued animal companion.**

Balder adopts the pup after discovering a Goliath woman who froze to death protecting it. The adoption honours her sacrifice and gives an early example of Balder’s compassion. Neither the pup nor its protector is named, and the recap does not record the pup’s subsequent activities.[^wolf]

### Yilthowral

**The spirit of a sage who once catalogued Icewind Dale’s flora.**

Yilthowral appears through the mystery of an unusually eloquent kobold and a pouch acquired by Balder. Despite his scholarly past, the spirit seeks power and status, briefly possessing Balder. The party resolves the encounter by burning the pouch and releasing the spirit; the recap does not describe a later return.[^yilthowral]

### Zathron

**A dark elf sorcerer associated with a possible larger threat.**

Zathron is introduced indirectly through a note found among the loot following the White Moose encounter. He is searching for a mysterious substance, suggesting a wider plot beyond the party’s immediate survival problems. The recap does not name the substance, explain his purpose or describe a direct encounter with him.[^zathron]

### Balder’s kobold followers

**An increasingly loyal entourage inspired by Balder’s divine display.**

These kobolds begin worshipping Balder after his Thaumaturgy-assisted performance on the lake. They follow Esmeraldor during the cave encounter, later identify him as the cauldron thief, and turn against him during the confrontation in East Haven. By the end of Session 3, Balder’s disciples have become minor local celebrities. No individual members are named.[^kobolds]

### Auril, the Frostmaiden

**The divine force behind the campaign’s central environmental crisis.**

The recap attributes Icewind Dale’s endless winter to Auril’s curse. Her influence creates the constant twilight, food shortages, lethal cold and dangerous conditions through which the party travels. She remains a background threat in these sessions rather than a directly encountered character.[^auril]

### Tyr

**The deity at the centre of Balder’s faith.**

Tyr is associated in the recap with Balder’s commitment to balance and justice. Balder invokes him during diplomacy, but the document does not describe Tyr appearing directly or issuing a personal instruction to the party.[^tyr]
