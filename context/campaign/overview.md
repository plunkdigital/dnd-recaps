# Icewind Dale: Rime of the Frostmaiden

User-confirmed campaign; user is a player, not the DM. Rules revision and house
rules are unconfirmed; do not infer this table's mechanics from the book's edition.

## Public premise

Icewind Dale is a frozen region of Faerûn. Ten-Towns are frontier communities
reliant on fishing and trade. Auril the Frostmaiden looms over a land caught in
endless winter. Cold, isolation, and scarcity form the backdrop, not evidence that
a particular storm or encounter happened in a recording.

Sources: [D&D Beyond introduction](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/834-what-are-ten-towns-the-frostmaiden)
and [setting overview](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/1886-running-winter-adventures-in-d-d),
reviewed 2026-10-16. Only the public premise is adopted; no adventure solutions.

## Our table

`characters.md` supplies Sessions 1–3 history; `state.md` indexes continuity and
`glossary.md` preserves table spellings. These override book canon for this campaign.
Do not reveal unseen places, secrets, motives, future events, or stat blocks.

Recaps: vivid third-person fantasy storytelling; about 150 words of opening and
600–1000 words of tale, shorter if sparse, plus a compact reference appendix.
Embellish prose, never dialogue, scenery, motives, actions, or outcomes beyond evidence.
Recordings/cloud agreement and retention preferences remain to be confirmed.
