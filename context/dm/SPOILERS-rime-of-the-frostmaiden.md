## Auril and later-adventure connections

The official [DM article](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/898-how-to-play-auril-the-frostmaiden-like-a-goddess)
portrays Auril as valuing frozen preservation over mortal life. Her desired stasis
is hidden motivation, not something to put into a player's narration as established fact.

It describes an encounter opportunity on Solstice, where the characters seek the
Codicil of White in Grimskalle. At night Auril may be away with her roc, Iskra,
maintaining the darkness. That is published encounter context, not a promise that
the table can or will follow a particular solution.

The article also identifies Ythryn as an ancient Netherese city preserved within
the glacier. Disturbing it can bring Auril into the city after the party. Its encounter
advice distinguishes three successive forms of Auril. Do not expose those phases,
abilities, vulnerabilities, or a tactical plan in player recaps.

The article contains optional author suggestions as well as descriptions of the
adventure. Alternative failure/recovery scenarios in it are suggestions, not events
that happened at this table. No stat blocks or tactical text are reproduced here.
